# Competitor Agent

## 1. Purpose & Responsibilities
The Competitor Agent analyzes the existing competitive landscape. It is responsible for identifying direct and indirect competitors, dissecting their pricing models, pinpointing their strengths and weaknesses, and discovering viable differentiation opportunities for the proposed startup idea.

## 2. Inputs & Outputs
- **Inputs**: 
  - The user's startup idea.
  - The shared research cache (`search_data`) generated by the Web Search Agent.
- **Outputs**: 
  - A JSON object detailing the Competitive Landscape Summary, Top Competitors (Company, Pricing, Strengths, Weaknesses, Key Features), General Market Strengths/Weaknesses, and Strategic Differentiation Opportunities.

## 3. Internal Workflow & Decision-Making Process
1. **Awaits Data**: Halts execution until `shared_context["search_data"]` is populated by the Web Search Agent.
2. **Context Extraction**: Isolates search snippets explicitly categorized under "Competitors".
3. **Analysis Generation**: Prompts the LLM to act as a cutthroat competitive strategist, objectively evaluating the incumbents based on the raw search evidence.
4. **Validation**: Passes output through `GuardrailManager` to ensure competitors listed actually exist within the provided context (preventing hallucinated companies).

## 4. Models & Tools Used
- **LLM**: Gemini or OpenRouter for competitive analysis and strategy formulation.

## 5. Backend & Frontend Integration
- **Backend Flow**: Executes concurrently alongside Market and Customer agents.
- **Frontend Flow**: Rendered in `CompetitorSection.jsx`, answering the pivotal question: "Can this startup realistically compete?" before listing competitor data.

## 6. Error Handling
If the search data reveals zero competitors (a true blue ocean, or a failed search), the agent is instructed to dynamically adapt its response to highlight the lack of direct incumbents as a distinct differentiation opportunity, rather than failing the parse.

## 7. Future Improvements
- Integrate with specialized financial APIs (e.g., Crunchbase) to append funding rounds and valuation data to the competitor profiles.
